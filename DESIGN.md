# DIMA Now UI system

Reviewed 2026-10-01 after the D-093 baseline and the D-094 improvement pass, and 2026-10-22 for the D-102 UX pass and the D-104 M3 Expressive alignment. Material 3 is the design authority; Apple HIG and the Toss consumer UX guide inform flows and wording where they do not contradict Material or Android behavior (D-102); Impeccable is a UX quality layer and its general web aesthetics do not override Material or Android behavior.

## Product tasks

Home answers what comes next: class, unfinished learning, departures, meals and notices. Timetable edits personal classes, term dates and no-class dates. Shuttle compares campus departures and the 4402 bus. Meals shows a selected day's services and supports a confirmed photo submission. Courses (수업) reads official LMS learning and details. 전체 (D-110) gathers what students open now and then: the timetable (kept mainly to drive class alerts and the Now Bar), 기숙사, 장비 대여, the DIMA portal and Settings. Settings controls location, guidance, Live Update/Now Bar, data sources and updates. First run shows what the app does, selects a return direction and asks for notifications and location in one step; the permissions that need a system settings page stay optional.

## Version boundary

Since D-103 the build uses Compose BOM 2026.09.00 (Compose 1.12.1) with `androidx.compose.material3:material3` pinned to 1.5.0-beta02, the first line that ships the M3 connected button group (`ToggleButton` with `ButtonGroupDefaults.connected*ButtonShapes`) without an experimental opt-in; this needs compileSdk 37, AGP 9.1.1 and Gradle 9.3.1 (targetSdk stays 36). Since D-104 the app uses the Expressive parts this line ships as stable: `MaterialExpressiveTheme` with `MotionScheme.expressive()`, the `*Emphasized` type roles, the increased shape slots, `ShortNavigationBar`, `WideNavigationRail`, `SegmentedListItem`, and the `ButtonDefaults.shapes()`/`IconButtonDefaults.shapes()` press morph. `LoadingIndicator` and `PullToRefreshDefaults.LoadingIndicator` are still experimental and are used behind an explicit `ExperimentalMaterial3ExpressiveApi` opt-in. Other screens keep stable components: `TopAppBar`, `ElevatedCard`, `FilterChip`, `RadioButton`, `DatePicker`, `TimePickerDialog` (with its dial/typed-entry toggle), `AlertDialog` and `PullToRefreshBox`. Do not present a hand-built control as a shipped Expressive component. Verified against the [release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/compose-material3).

## Code layout

Package `com.example.dimanow.ui`: `DimaNowApp.kt` (root, tabs, routes), `HomeScreen.kt`, `TimetableScreen.kt` + `TimetableDialogs.kt`, `ShuttleScreen.kt` + `Bus4402Screen.kt`, `MealScreen.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`, and shared parts (`ScreenScaffold.kt`, `DesignComponents.kt`, `SettingsChoice.kt`, `TimetableChips.kt`, `DataLoadState.kt`, `StateMessages.kt`, `DateLabels.kt`, `SystemIntents.kt`, `GuidanceSetup.kt` for permission state, `LocationAccess.kt` for in-context location requests, `HomeUpdateNotice.kt`; `@Preview`s in `ComponentPreviews.kt`). Motion lives in `ui/motion`, dialogs in `ui/schedule`, first run in `ui/onboarding`, tokens in `theme/`. The LMS UI is split into `LmsScreen.kt` (route), `LmsItemsScreen.kt`, `LmsDetailScreen.kt`, `LmsLoginScreen.kt`, `LmsOfficialCourseWebView.kt` and `LmsWebViewSupport.kt`.

## Tokens

- **Color**: the fixed DIMA magenta light/dark schemes (D-024, D-027), not wallpaper color; D-083 role assignments stand except for last service. Surfaces come from four app tokens (D-114, `DimaTheme.colors`): `page` (light `surfaceContainerLow`, dark `surface`), `card` (light `surfaceContainerLowest` white, dark `surfaceContainerHigh`), `cardInner` for badges, tiles and unselected toggle buttons (light `surfaceContainerHigh`, dark `surfaceContainerHighest`) and `scrolledHeader` (`surfaceContainer`), so a card stands clearly apart from the page in both themes, as list rows do in the system Settings app; the navigation bar and rail use their M3 defaults. Error roles mean errors only: last-service departures use the app's static warning color (`DimaTheme.colors.warning`, `onWarning`, `warningContainer`, `onWarningContainer`; amber, light and dark; D-104), in the app and the widgets. Tertiary (warm gold) marks contrasting accents: the `오늘` badge, the first departure and the next-class badge on Home.
- **Shape** (`DimaShapes` over the M3 slots): `Card` 20dp (`largeIncreased`) for every card or section container, `Tile` 12dp for departure capsules, inset panels and selector cells, `Badge` 8dp for tags and status chips, `Dialog` 28dp (extra large, the AlertDialog default); D-105 restored these after D-104's 12/8dp. The theme also sets `extraLargeIncreased` 32dp and `extraExtraLarge` 48dp. Buttons keep their full Material shape and morph on press; pills use `CircleShape`. No `RoundedCornerShape(n.dp)` literals in screens.
- **Type**: the M3 baseline sizes and the official `*Emphasized` roles (D-104) with heavier weights since D-118: body Medium (500, emphasis Bold), titles and headlines Bold (700, emphasis ExtraBold 800), labels SemiBold (600, emphasis ExtraBold); display keeps its baseline weights. Every role uses the Korean (medium language-height) line heights: display 73/56/47, headline 42/38/35, title 31/27/23, body 27/23/18, label 23/18/18sp. Emphasized roles are reserved for screen titles, next-departure countdowns, the primary Home briefing line and selected state.
- **Motion** (`DimaMotion`, the only motion tokens, read from `MotionScheme.expressive()`): *spatial* springs (fast 0.6/800, default 0.8/380, slow 0.8/200 damping/stiffness) move or resize with a visible overshoot; *effects* springs (damping 1; fast 3800, default 1600, slow 800) change color or opacity without overshoot. Destinations change with an expressive fade-through: the outgoing page fades on the fast effects spring while the incoming page fades in on the default one, so the old page is gone before the new one is opaque. A list rises 12dp only on its first appearance; after a tab switch the transition is the only motion. Press feedback (`expressiveBounceClick`) scales to 0.97 and is dropped when system animations are off. Persistent status never pulses.

## Structure and components

- **App bar**: every primary screen, Settings and Courses use `ScreenScaffold` with one pinned small `DimaTopAppBar` (headlineMediumEmphasized title, ExtraBold, D-118 superseding D-106's baseline weight; `surface` tinting to `surfaceContainer` once content scrolls under it). D-105 removed D-104's large collapsing title: one header row, no second row of empty space. An optional sub-header (mode switch, day selector) is pinned beneath and tints with it. No screen carries a settings gear any more (D-110): Settings opens from 전체. Pages opened from 전체 (시간표, 기숙사, 장비 대여, 설정) have a leading Back action and no navigation bar; Back returns to 전체, or to Home for the timetable opened from Home's 수업 추가하기/새 학기 설정하기. Home's title is the semester week (`9주차`, `9주차 · 중간고사`, `방학 · 개강까지 12일`) with today's date beside it in a smaller titleMedium (D-106). Meals and Shuttle pin their mode switch and day selector. Timetable adds classes with the M3 Expressive FAB menu once it has courses (D-111: the toggle FAB opens 직접 추가 and 인트라넷에서 가져오기; without the school bridge it is the plain extended FAB); an empty timetable offers the same two ways in its empty state instead.
- **Content** is a `LazyColumn` of keyed items 12dp apart (16dp between cards and sections) with 16dp side margins; on windows wider than the 840dp reading width the column centers, and the app bar and its pinned sub-header line up with it over a full-width header surface, while the list still spans the window, so it scrolls and refreshes from anywhere (D-104). Pull-to-refresh only where a source can refresh, with the Expressive loading indicator and always a matching refresh action in the app bar (`셔틀 새로고침`, `식단 새로고침`, `수업 새로고침`). Tabs keep their scroll state through a saveable state holder. The LMS detail pane and onboarding cap reading width at 840dp. Loading uses the Expressive `LoadingIndicator`.
- **Navigation**: five destinations (홈, 셔틀, 식단, 수업, 전체; D-110 replaced 시간표 with the Toss-style 전체 at the end). 전체 is a page of M3 Expressive segmented lists grouped as 학교 생활 (시간표, 기숙사 with the saved points, 장비 대여), 바로가기 (DIMA 포털, in the browser) and 앱 (설정; 앱 정보 with updates and 이 앱에 대해, moved out of 설정; 문의·오류 제보, a full-screen form the gateway posts as a public GitHub issue — D-119). Below 600dp window width they sit in the Expressive `ShortNavigationBar`; from 600dp (tablets, unfolded foldables) in a collapsed `WideNavigationRail` at the start edge (D-104). The active indicator and a filled icon mark selection (other destinations use outlined icons, one label weight). Selecting the current destination again scrolls it to the top. Phones are portrait-only (D-054); large screens (smallest width 600dp+) follow the device's rotation (D-104).
- **Icon badges** (D-114): card headers (`HomeCardHeader`, 32dp) and list rows (40dp) put their icon in a `secondaryContainer` circle (`DimaIconBadge`), so each card and row reads as its own item; the icon is decorative and the text names the item.
- **Cards**: elevated cards in the `card` tone with 20dp corners and inner tiles in `cardInner` (D-114) (D-079/D-081, restored by D-105 after D-104 tried filled 12dp cards). A card is either one tap target or a container of its own controls, never both (D-102). Home summaries are whole-card taps (`HomeSummaryCard`) with a spoken click label; a Home card that needs an action, such as the location request, is a `HomeContainerCard` with buttons and no chevron. Notices share one card with individually actionable rows. Settings use cards grouped under section headings; the 앱 정보 links and the permission checklist are M3 Expressive segmented lists (`DimaSegmentedList`, `SegmentedListItem`: 2dp gaps, large outer and small inner corners) with a leading icon and a trailing open-in-browser or chevron mark, check mark or action.
- **School sites** (D-107, D-108): intranet and DIMA C go through one shell-level host (`school/SchoolServiceHost.kt`) that lies over the whole window, navigation bar and rail included. For a read, an invisible WebView opens the page, signs in through the portal (the intranet via its own `/sso/main.aspx` entry) with the saved portal account or, when none is saved, asks for it on the same sign-in form as the Courses tab (titled 학교 계정 로그인, optional saving, close X and Back cancel), and runs a page script. For a page the student uses (외출·외박 신청, DIMA C 기자재 대여신청) the host opens full screen at once: a loading state while signing in, then the signed-in school page under one bar with the page title and a close X; Back steps back within the page first, links off the school sites open in the browser, pinch zoom is on, intranet pages hide the intranet's side menu and header, and tables keep the school's layout (scrolling sideways, never reflowed into cards). The official portal page is shown only for a portal challenge or the duplicate-login notice. Reads start only from a screen the student opened or a button: timetable import (top-bar action and an empty-timetable prompt, then a preview dialog; each course's shuttle side follows its room's building, and only a room in an unknown building asks 본관/엔터관), 기숙사 (a page of its own under 전체, D-110/D-111: 상점 and 벌점 side by side, this term's entries as a segmented list, 외출·외박 신청 as its own row; pull-to-refresh and the bar's refresh action; the last 상벌점 read is saved on the device with `오늘 02:05 기준`; 외출·외박 신청 opens in the app), 장비 대여 (a page under 전체, D-110: a 대여 안내 card with three key points, 자세히 보기 for the full notice, a link to the term's notice search and DIMA C에서 신청하기; then 일반 대여 장비 groups as segmented lists whose open model shows a Monday–Friday grid of free units, 마감, 휴일 and 오픈 전). Equipment results live in memory only; an imported timetable and the last 상벌점 read stay on the device.
- **기숙사** (D-110–D-113): the first screen keeps the daily things only — 오늘 (점호 from KakaoTalk announcements read through notification access, else the Tuesday 23:00 rule with holidays and exam weeks noted; the front door 06:00–02:00; while notices are not read, one text button `카톡 공지로 점호 확인하기` opens a dialog with one paragraph on what is read and kept and a single `설정 열기`), a whole-card 상벌점 summary that opens the 상벌점 page, the 외출·외박 신청 row, and a 생활 정보 segmented list whose rows open pages for 시설 이용 시간 (open-now badges), 택배 (address copy), 식권·식당, 생활 규칙과 입·퇴사 and 공지와 수칙. Pages open over the first screen with Back; the source line sits on each page. Facts come from the 2026-2 orientation material (`ui/DormGuide.kt`).
- **수업** (D-117): modes are `할 일` and `과목별` (not 오늘/전체, which clashed with the 전체 tab). 할 일 lists every deadline still ahead (nearest first), then undated news, then missed deadlines (most recently missed first), then `완료한 학습` folded under one `SettingsExpander`; 과목별 groups by course in catalog order with the 종류/과목 dropdown chips (D-058). Each section is a heading in titleLarge on the text color with its count in primary beside it (`다가오는 마감 2개`, D-118) over an M3 Expressive segmented list on the `card` tone. Under 과목별 each course shows what is left (or `남은 학습이 없어요` in quiet text), then `기한 지남` and `완료한 학습` folded, each opening with the expressive spatial spring and a fade (`AnimatedVisibility`, D-118). A row has the kind as a `DimaIconBadge`, the title in titleMedium (two lines at most), `새 항목 · 과제 · 과목` with the course Bold on the text color (course left out under 과목별) and the exact deadline (`10월 19일 (월) 23:59 마감`) or post date; on the right, how much time is left (`20분 남음`, `오늘 23:59`, `내일 18:00`, `3일 남음`, `4일 지남`), in the warning color and emphasized only within about two days. Rows no longer repeat 미완료/완료. A fold opens from a slim one-line row (`LmsFoldHeader`: muted tone, thin outlineVariant border, 14dp corners, a history or check icon, the count and a turning chevron), so it reads apart from the cards (D-120). Folded rows (기한 지남, 완료한 학습) sit on the quieter `cardMuted` tone with onSurfaceVariant text and a surfaceVariant icon circle (D-119). A quiet line above says when the list was read (`오늘 22:21에 불러왔어요`).
- **Pop-ups** (D-120): every alert has a 56dp tone circle above its title (`DimaDialogIcon`): `DialogTone.WARNING` in the warning container for "this may not work", `DANGER` in the error container for "this removes something" (its confirm label in error), `INFO` in secondaryContainer otherwise. Titles say what happened or what is asked; bodies are one or two plain sentences; at most two text buttons. `DimaAlertDialog` and `DialogTimeline` (labelled times with arrows, the current one in the tone's container) are the shared pieces.
- **외출·외박 신청** (D-119, D-120): before the intranet page opens, an alert asks when applying may not count: on a 점호 day from the deadline (21:00, or an announced time such as 20:59 inclusive) — `오늘 신청 시간이 지났어요` — and from 23:59 until the front door opens at 06:00 — `외박 신청 시간이 지났어요`; `신청 화면 열기` still opens it, `닫기` stays.
- **Academic schedule** (D-107): Home's 학사 일정 card lists periods running today or starting within two weeks (exam weeks included) with a tertiary `진행 중`/`오늘까지` badge or a quieter `D-n`; a morning reminder (own channel, 알림 종류 → 학사 일정) says when one starts, ends or an exam week begins tomorrow.
- **Forms**: adding or editing a class is a full-screen dialog (close X, `저장`, edge-to-edge) with the fields in reading order, inline errors, keyboard next/done and a discard confirmation; times use the time picker dialog following the phone's 12/24-hour setting.
- **Permissions**: ask where the feature needs it, with one sentence on why; first run asks for notifications and location through one `계속`, Home asks for location inside the shuttle card, and Settings summarises what is still off. Buttons say what happens: `허용하기` (Android prompt) or `설정 열기` (system page). Refusal never blocks the app.
- **Single choice**: the M3 connected button group (`DimaConnectedChoice`: equal-width toggle buttons, the selected one filled and fully rounded, read as radio items) for the meal venue, shuttle view, Courses mode and `SettingsChoice` options whose labels fit; otherwise radio rows; both at least 48dp tall. Expandable sections use `SettingsExpander` with expanded state and expand/collapse actions. Dialog actions are text buttons; destructive confirms use error-colored text.
- **Day/date selectors** (`DimaDaySelector`, `DimaDateSelector`): equal-width `FilterChip` cells with the weekday initial, the day of month for dates, and a today dot; cells are ≥52dp tall, announce as tabs with `오늘`/`오늘, 선택됨` state, and wrap into balanced rows on narrow widths or large text. Term and no-class dates use Material `DatePicker` dialogs.
- **Departures**: `DepartureCapsule` puts the countdown/service label on one line and the clock beneath, for Home, the campus shuttle and 4402 alike, in tabular figures so ticking digits keep their width. The full timetable is an always-visible horizontal `TimetableChipRow` of plain time labels, not chips, because the times cannot be tapped (D-104): each is ≥32dp tall; text color marks next (primary, emphasized), first (tertiary), last (warning) and past (the quieter variant color, still readable); every time has a spoken description such as `다음 출발 21:30` or `21:50, 막차`, so meaning never depends on color alone. Next-departure capsules fill with primary for the nearest departure, `warning` when that is the last service, `warningContainer` for a later last service and otherwise `secondaryContainer`.
- **Meals**: the selected date stays pinned through automatic service focus; operating hours carry emphasis over a quiet meal surface, and a closed meal card uses surface tokens in both themes. Origin notes remain as supporting text.
- **Widgets**: below 300dp width or at 180dp times the font scale in height, summary widgets use a flat three-summary layout; larger widgets keep separated sections. Ellipsized summaries keep complete accessibility descriptions.
- **Now Bar chip** (D-061, D-109): the Live Update status chip is short static text reposted at each minute: a shuttle names its destination (`본관행 8분`), a class its course short name (`방시전 12분`, `방시전 곧`, `방시전 수업 중`). The short name takes the first syllable of each word, up to three (`domain/CourseAbbreviation.kt`, a small course-word vocabulary because names are written without spaces). The classroom chip option shows the room and keeps the system timer in the card.
- **Countdowns**: minutes are shown only within 60 minutes (`12분 후`, `시작까지 12분`); further away the clock is named (`다음 출발`, `13:00 시작`, `내일 13:00 시작`).
- **States**: a source that has not emitted yet shows `LoadingLine`, never an empty or error message. `EmptyState` explains an empty result with the actions that fill it (a primary and an optional quieter one); `ErrorState` says what failed in plain words with a retry where the screen can refresh. Refresh snackbars appear only after a user-initiated refresh; undo snackbars follow no-class deletion; a short snackbar confirms where new classes went. Nothing opens over the app at launch: a new version waits as a Home card.

## Content and voice

Use `홈`, `시간표`, `셔틀`, `식단`, `수업`, `설정`. User-visible stops are `엔터관`, `본관`, `원룸촌`, with `운동장` when the departure boards at the stadium stop (D-090). All copy uses one polite 해요체 voice, active verbs (`바뀌었어요`, not `변경됐어요`), `돼요` rather than `되어요`, and says what makes something possible rather than only what is missing. Buttons name their result; dialog dismiss buttons stay `취소`. Errors name the failed operation and a recovery; technical diagnostics stay in Settings. Dates read as `9월 30일 (수)`, adding the year only when it differs. Preserve complete learning and meal content, first/last departure facts, protected deletion, photo-sharing confirmation, optional permissions and Android Back behavior.

## Accessibility

Touch targets are at least 48dp. Text and dense data grow with the system font size instead of clipping. Selection, today, expanded and past states are exposed to screen readers, and color is never the only signal. Decorative motion stops when system animations are removed.
